QuantumChem · University of St. ThomasMobile app directions

Two mobile directions for QuantumChem — an interactive quantum chemistry learning app for undergraduates.

The existing web app has a strong foundation (St. Thomas purple, Lora + Lato, a dense simulations library). For the phone experience I'm evolving the type, tightening the visual language, and proposing two nav approaches you can pick between. Both keep your content model intact — categories, simulations, and per-sim dashboards with controls, canvas and graph — just rearranged for a 402 × 874 canvas.

Design system

Shared ground across both directions, then the two split on type, nav, and sim‑control layout.

Shared

  • St. Thomas Purple #340E51 as primary; warm paper whites, no saturated whites.
  • Breadcrumb always visible — students often jump mid‑topic.
  • Three‑level information: Categories → Simulation → Controls + Canvas + Graph.
  • KaTeX inline for the equations that matter per sim.
  • Respect the existing 14 weeks of lecture content as a first‑class surface.

Direction A · Editorial textbook

  • Serif‑forward — Fraunces display + Newsreader body + JetBrains Mono meta.
  • Sidebar / drawer nav with collapsible chapters.
  • Left stack of controls, big canvas on a continuous scroll — reads like a lab‑notebook page.

Direction B · Lab instrument

  • Sans + Mono — IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono; numbers in mono always.
  • Top breadcrumb + tab bar; no sidebar, fewer taps.
  • Canvas is the hero; controls live in a pinned bottom sheet you can drag.
Direction A

Editorial textbook

Fraunces · Newsreader · JetBrains Mono
Drawer nav · left‑rail controls

Reads like a well‑set physical‑chemistry textbook. The whole interior of the app is one long breadcrumb trail — chapter, topic, simulation — and simulations open as continuous scrolling "pages" with controls, canvas, and graph stacked vertically. Good for learners who read linearly and want the lecture notes adjacent to the sim.

Direction B

Lab instrument

IBM Plex Sans · Plex Mono · Plex Serif
Tab bar · bottom‑sheet controls

Pragmatic, dashboard‑y. The simulation canvas is the hero and takes the full width; controls live in a pinned bottom sheet that you drag up to see more, collapse to see less. Numbers render in Plex Mono so readouts feel scientific. Navigation is a permanent breadcrumb + a tab bar, so students can dart between Sim / Notes / Equations without losing place.
